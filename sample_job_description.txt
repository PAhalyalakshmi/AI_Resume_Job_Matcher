
JOB TITLE: MIS Executive / Junior Data Analyst

JOB DESCRIPTION:

We are looking for a motivated MIS Executive who can support
business reporting and data management activities.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

1. Prepare daily, weekly, and monthly MIS reports.
2. Maintain Excel spreadsheets and business databases.
3. Perform data cleaning and data validation.
4. Create dashboards and visual reports.
5. Analyze business data and identify trends.
6. Support management decision-making.
7. Maintain accurate and updated records.
8. Automate repetitive reporting tasks.
9. Work with cross-functional teams.
